Nothing complicated... just a little surprise. 🌷

Dear H,


I honestly don’t know exactly when it started. Maybe it was when I first saw you, with your long hair flowing naturally. There was something simple about you, pero somehow, you caught my attention.


At first, I thought it was just admiration. I admired the way you carry yourself, the way you speak, and the kindness that you show to other people. But habang mas nakikilala kita, I realized na hindi na pala basta paghanga lang.


I slowly fell for you. 💚


Hindi lang dahil maganda ka. It’s because there’s something different about you. You’re a woman who loves God, and that’s one of the things I admire most about you. Seeing your faith, your heart, and the way you try to live for Him makes me respect you even more.


Your glasses, your long hair, your smile, and the little things you probably don’t even notice— somehow, those things became memorable to me. Pero higit sa lahat, it’s the person behind those things that made me appreciate you.


I’m not writing this to pressure you or expect anything from you. I just wanted to be honest about what I feel.


Somewhere along the way, without even planning it, you became someone special to me.


Maybe I fell for you slowly through the little moments, the conversations, and the things I noticed about you. And if I’m being completely honest, I’m thankful that I got the chance to know someone like you.


A woman with a beautiful heart, a strong faith, and a love for God.


I don’t know where this feeling will lead, but for now, I just want you to know one thing:


I’m glad I met you. 💚

— From someone who slowly fell for you
